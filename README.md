# Archdesign Wheel

A round app launcher for Android that floats on top of every app.

- Drag the bubble anywhere on the screen.
- Tap it to open the wheel of apps; tap an app to open it, tap anywhere else to close.
- Long-press the bubble, or tap EDIT APPS in the middle of the wheel, to choose the apps.

## Getting the app on your phone
GitHub builds the app automatically every time this project changes.
Open the **Releases** section of this project on your phone, tap **ArchdesignWheel.apk**, then open the download to install it.
